# Presetify for RuneScape via Alt1

**Presetify lets you customize and overlay images onto your RuneScape bank presets inside Alt1, giving your setups a clean, personalized, and visually enhanced look—perfect for organization, skilling, PvM, or just showing off your style.**

## Features
- **Upload and overlay any image** directly onto your bank preset layout.
- **Preview changes live** inside Alt1 before saving your design.
- **Supports transparent PNGs** for clean, polished presets.
- **Lightweight, fast, and designed specifically for RuneScape players.**

## Why Use Presetify?
Your presets are the heart of your gameplay. Presetify helps you:
- Highlight important presets  
- Create themed presets (PvM, skilling, clue hunting, PvP, etc.)  
- Add icons for organization  
- Make your setup visually unique  
- Share clean-looking presets with friends or clanmates  

No more guessing where items should go—add style, clarity, and personalization.

## Installation
1. Visit the plugin [here](https://presetify.unlishema.org/appconfig.json).  
2. Click **Add App**.  
4. Launch Presetify anytime through the Alt1 app panel.

## Usage
- Import an image from your device.  
- Save or reset your design instantly.  
- Works seamlessly with RuneScape’s interface scaling.

## Compatibility
- **RuneScape 3 (NXT client)**  
- **Windows 10/11**  
- **Alt1 Toolkit 1.8+ recommended**

## Development
Presetify was created with:
